Transliflow is an AI-powered collaborative translation platform: teams bring source copy, get AI-drafted translations, review together, and export localized strings. Built with Next.js, Prisma and MySQL, it scratched my own itch localizing ekilie products for East African markets.

Live at [transliflow.vercel.app](https://transliflow.vercel.app). Related ecosystem work: [ekiliRelay](/projects/ekilirelay/) and [Magreth](/projects/magreth/).
