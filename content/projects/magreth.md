Magreth is an AI therapist and friendly companion: a calm web app for supportive conversations, guided reflection and journaling. It is part of the [ekilie](https://ekilie.com) ecosystem, sending mail through [ekiliRelay](/projects/ekilirelay/).

## What it does

- Supportive AI conversations with memory of your journey
- Journaling and mood reflection tools
- Private by design, hosted on ekilie infrastructure

Live at [magreth.ekilie.com](https://magreth.ekilie.com).
